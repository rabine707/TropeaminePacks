# Treatment Preview v1

Prototype spec for the Tropeamine Packs card-treatment system. This branch is intentionally isolated from production while the visual treatments are tuned.

## Treatments

- Base — original uploaded card artwork, untouched.
- Holo — existing rainbow foil treatment and common slot-five pull.
- Heartthrob — transparent repeating holographic hearts; hearts catch rainbow/chrome light without tinting the underlying card pink.
- Unhinged — irregular shattered-glass refractor facets; no literal crack artwork, with independently shifting prismatic facets.
- Slow Burn — restrained warm ember treatment; mostly transparent at rest with a warm reflective bloom that travels upward during interaction.
- Aftercare — soft premium pearl/satin iridescence; broad silky light bands with champagne/pearl/rainbow reflections.

## Interaction

Treatments are display layers over the finished card artwork. They must preserve the baked-in card borders, branding, names, and readable text. Pointer/touch position should drive the reflective highlight. Mobile device tilt can be considered later.

The eventual card-detail selector is:

Base | Holo | Heartthrob | Unhinged | Slow Burn | Aftercare

## Economy locked for implementation after visual approval

Slot 5: Holo 70%, Heartthrob 14%, Unhinged 8%, Slow Burn 5%, Aftercare 3%.

Treatments are permanent per-character unlocks, not inventory quantities. Repeated treatment pulls convert to character-specific Duplicates, separate from account Shards.

Craft costs in Duplicates: Holo 3, Heartthrob 5, Unhinged 10, Slow Burn 15, Aftercare 25.

Duplicate treatment conversion: Holo +1, Heartthrob +2, Unhinged +3, Slow Burn +4, Aftercare +6.
