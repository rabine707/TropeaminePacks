# Published beta catalog review

Reviewed October 14, 2026 using read-only published catalog metadata and downloaded browse-size front/back images: 67 SFW cards, 134 assets, nine series. Originals were preserved. This is a visual/content inventory, not legal clearance or a full-resolution review of every small-print label.

The creator confirmed that all artwork is fan-made and no author/publisher permissions have been obtained. Every entry below remains **rights unconfirmed; no permission obtained**. A fan-made disclaimer, free distribution, and non-explicit artwork do not establish permission. Before broader distribution, obtain appropriate permission/licensing or replace disputed material with original, cleared work; have qualified counsel assess unresolved uses.

No visible nudity or depicted sexual acts were observed in the contact sheets. Several cards still have sexual references, cleavage or lingerie-like clothing, violent imagery, and darker themes. The rating `sfw` is an existing catalog label, not an independent certification of suitability for all ages.

Review flags:

- Coven King, Dungeon Diving, and Warlock: suggestive clothing and mature fantasy themes deserve an individual creator review.
- Back-card text includes lust/kink and other mature references; metadata includes violence, stalking and adult occupations. Use the mature-theme notice throughout tester onboarding.
- Rowan has a bloodied shirt; avoid presenting the beta as free of violent imagery.
- **Lady Rendral** is the database name, but its artwork reads **Elysara**. Confirm intended identity before wider release; this review did not alter the assets.
- Book/series names, author names, character identities and logos require review alongside the illustrations. Removing nudity does not address those rights questions.
- The original source books can be much more explicit than the cards. This review makes no claim about the books' suitability or legal classification.
